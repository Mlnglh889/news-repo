World news, Wednesday 11 November 2026: US-mediated talks between Russia and Ukraine continued in Abu Dhabi for a second day, according to the BBC, while world leaders began speaking at the COP31 climate summit in Antalya, Turkey (AFP). In Washington, the White House announced a new Indo-Pacific maritime-security package with Japan, Australia and the Philippines; Reuters reported that the agreement includes expanded joint exercises and coast-guard patrols. India’s Election Commission released the final results of the Assam state election, with the BJP-led alliance winning a majority, according to The Hindu. In Europe, the European Commission proposed a revised 2035 automotive-emissions timetable that would allow additional low-emission hybrids; the Financial Times reported that Germany and Italy backed the change while environmental groups opposed it. In Sudan, the UN said fighting around El Fasher had displaced more civilians and restricted aid deliveries, Reuters reported. Japan’s Cabinet approved a supplementary economic package focused on household energy costs and semiconductor investment, according to NHK. China’s commerce ministry said it would review new US export restrictions on advanced computing components, Xinhua reported. In the Americas, Mexico’s Senate passed a measure creating a national missing-persons investigation unit after a cross-party vote, according to AP. Scientists at CERN reported a new precision measurement of the muon magnetic moment, saying the result narrows but does not resolve the discrepancy with the Standard Model. In sports, the ATP Finals group stage continued in Turin, while the WTA Finals qualification race reached its final week.
